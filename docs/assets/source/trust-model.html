Evidence and trust model

Every statement AgentOps shows carries one of four labels and cites the evidence items it rests on.

Observed
Read directly from recorded data.
May cite observed evidence only.
Example
Revenue for 2026-08: SGD 5,752,877.
What was revenue last month?
→
Calculated
Computed from recorded data by a registered calculation.
Its tool result must have succeeded; stated numbers must match it.
Example
The largest contribution to the revenue decline by region came from APAC: revenue changed by -SGD 62,570 (-2.74%) from 2026-07 to 2026-08; 92.1% of the gross decline.
Which region had the largest revenue decline last month?
→
Inferred
A reading of the evidence, not an observed fact.
Not a cause: causal wording (“caused by”, “due to”) is rejected.
Example
The available evidence indicates the revenue decline was concentrated in region APAC, which accounted for 92.1% of the gross decline.
Same question
→
Recommended
A suggested next step, not a finding.
Cites the validated findings it rests on.
Example
Investigate the country Singapore accounts within region APAC behind the revenue decline, for example their churn and contraction in 2026-08.
Rests on F16 (calculated), F17 (inferred). Investigation: Why is revenue growth slowing?
closer to the recorded datamore interpretation

Checked for every claim before it is shown

  • It cites evidence from a successful tool call, with provenance (query IDs, source tables, calculation).
  • Its numbers and direction match the cited evidence.
  • Its metric, period, dimension and unit match the evidence (claim/evidence identity).
  • It uses no causal wording. Investigation drivers are typed as accounting shares, co-movements, associations or contradictions, never as causes.
  • A claim that fails is removed, never shown.
Labels and notes as shown in the UI. Examples are real output on the default dataset (seed 42, data as of 2026-08-31).
